# User Guide

Follow these steps to set up and use the Travel Buddy mobile application.

1. Install the mobile application

   - Install from the Play Store (if available) or install the provided APK on your Android device.

2. Fill basic information about yourself

   - Open the app and complete your profile (name, phone number, email).

3. Add Emergency Contacts

   - Add one or more emergency contacts in the Emergency Contacts screen.
   - Make sure to enter the correct email address for each contact so they can receive and accept requests.

4. Ask your contacts to install and accept

   - Ask each contact to download the app and create an account.
   - They should check their email and accept your contact request so they can receive your trip notifications.

5. Give permissions

   - Allow the app to send notifications (push) and access location.
   - Notifications: required to receive alerts and SOS messages.
   - Location: required for live tracking and stationary detection.

6. Start a trip and select contacts

   - When starting a trip, select one or more contacts to share your trip with.

7. Contacts receive notifications and can view live location

   - Selected contacts are notified that your trip started and can view your live location in the app.

8. Stationary detection

   - If you remain in the same location for more than 5 minutes, the app will show a dialog asking “Are you okay?”.
   - If you do not respond within 10 seconds, the app will automatically send a notification to your emergency contacts.

9. SOS (dangerous situation)

   - If you are in danger, press the SOS button in the app.
   - The app will immediately notify your selected contacts.

10. Complete trip
    - After your trip, press the Complete Trip button to end the trip and stop sharing live location.

Troubleshooting & Tips

- Ensure your contacts accept your request and have the correct email address.
- Keep location and notifications enabled for reliable tracking and alerts.
- If notifications are not received, ask contacts to check spam/junk mail for the invitation email.
- Keep the app updated to get the latest safety improvements.
